Beginner Practice Drills

Batting Drill: Straight Drive

  1. Keep your feet comfortably apart and eyes level.
  2. Watch the ball closely from the bowler's hand.
  3. Move your front foot towards the ball.
  4. Play a controlled, straight shot and maintain balance.
  5. Repeat slowly, focusing on technique rather than power.

Fielding Drill

  1. Get into a low ready position.
  2. Move your feet behind the rolling ball.
  3. Collect it with soft hands.
  4. Throw towards a safe, clear target.

Cricket Fitness

Warm-up

Start with 5–10 minutes of easy movement and dynamic stretches.

Agility

Practise controlled side steps and short accelerations in a safe space.

Recovery

Drink water, rest between sessions and increase training gradually.

Tips to Improve

  • Practise regularly and focus on one skill at a time.
  • Master technique before trying to hit harder or bowl faster.
  • Keep a notebook of your practice and progress.
  • Learn from a qualified coach whenever possible.
